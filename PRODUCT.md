# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are tenant admins, business owners, accountants, sales users, and viewers working inside a multi-company CRM and billing SaaS. They manage sensitive customer, quote, invoice, payment, and reconciliation workflows for DFM entities first, then future external tenants.

## Product Purpose

COMPTE is a multi-tenant SaaS for customers, quotes, invoices, payment tracking, and bank reconciliation. It replaces MEG for DFM-Europe first, then becomes a sellable platform for other tenants. Success means users can operate daily CRM and billing workflows with proper roles, company separation, auditability, and automation.

## Positioning

The product combines CRM, Devis, Facture, payment reconciliation, role-based access, tenant isolation, and AI-assisted document/email workflows in one operational workspace designed for French/EU billing constraints.

## Operating Context

The app supports tenants, legal entities, role scopes, quote-to-invoice lifecycle, invoice validation locks, payment reconciliation, reminders, document numbering, templates, and multilingual work in FR/EN/VI. The current frontend is a Next.js App Router codebase.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Phase 1 includes login and account access, tenant/company administration, dashboard, customers, product catalog, quotes, quote lists, invoices, email/PDF templates, notifications, audit/security surfaces, and platform administration foundations. Backend integration is not present in the current frontend foundation.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: COMPTE. The prototype visual language is a serious B2B workspace: restrained blue accent, dense but calm layouts, tables, forms, status badges, and French business-document terminology.

## Product Principles

- Tenant and company boundaries are visible and trustworthy.
- Financial documents prioritize clarity, auditability, and irreversible-state warnings.
- Daily workflows are compact, fast to scan, and role-aware.
- AI and automation propose important actions, while users keep control over external effects.
- UI copy avoids fabricated claims and labels demo/sample data honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web interface must remain keyboard accessible, responsive for desktop and tablet, and prepared for FR/EN/VI content.
